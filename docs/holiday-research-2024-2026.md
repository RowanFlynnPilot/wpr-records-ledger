# Office-closure holidays, 2024–2026: six central Wisconsin records custodians

Researched 2026-10-23. Every rule below comes from the agency's own published document. Third-party material (the Wisconsin circuit court closure schedules) is used only to corroborate a date, and is labeled where it appears.

**How dates are marked**
- **[R]**: the date itself was read in an agency source: a calendar entry, a closure notice, or a newsletter.
- **[I]**: the date was computed from the agency's written holiday rule and weekend-observance rule. No dated notice was found for it.
- **[U]**: undetermined. The source says the day is set case by case, and no notice was found.

**Day-of-week facts behind the inferences**
- 2023-12-31 (New Year's Eve) was a Sunday, so its Monday observance collides with New Year's Day, 2024-01-01.
- 2026-07-04 is a Saturday.
- Every other listed holiday in 2024–2026 falls on a weekday.
