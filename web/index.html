The Records Ledger

How central Wisconsin agencies answer the public records requests Wausau Pilot & Review files: how long they hold a request, what they charge, what they deny, and whether their denials follow the law.

One card per agency, in alphabetical order; nothing is ranked. A median or share appears only when it rests on at least 5 requests, and the number it rests on is always shown.

Quarterly audit

Each quarter, WPR files the same simple request, in identical wording, with every records custodian the Closed Door Ledger covers, so the answers can be compared directly. A round appears here once it is published.
